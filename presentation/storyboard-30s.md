## Scientific boundaries

The chatbot is presented as research assistance for evidence and uncertainty, with no claim of clinical validation or causal meal/exercise advice. Wearable data are terrestrial; ground-analogue cardiac data remain visit-based; ISS radiation is environmental instrument exposure. Independent sources are not synchronized observations of one astronaut. The denser final timeline is a data-collection goal, not a demonstrated improvement in accuracy.
