# Research data

Raw data archives and the private local activity journal are not distributed. The repository includes a compact, transformed research bundle under `outputs/presentation_campaign/` and `outputs/source_campaign_v3/`, plus a fixed public replay under `docs/data/`.

BIG IDEAs-derived series are date-shifted/de-identified public research data, aggregated into 15-minute bins with missingness retained. Attribution and the original ODC-By license are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `third_party/`. NASA cardiac and environmental sources retain their distinct study identities and original cadence. The sources and transformations are documented in the manifests and acquisition/audit scripts.

For historical analyses, reacquire the linked public source data using the corresponding scripts in `scripts/`. Large earlier UCI/PhysioNet, NASA assay and research-expansion workspaces are deliberately not included in this Git repository. No browser profile, personal CLI login or private journal is part of the publication.
