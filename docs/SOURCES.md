# Data sources and resources

Independent studies are not synchronized measurements of one astronaut. NASA cardiovascular studies are ground analogues; ISS radiation is environmental instrument data; the glucose/pulse demonstration is terrestrial.

## NASA OSD-575 — Inspiration4 metabolic and biomarker research

https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575

Earlier exploratory astronaut biomarker model; sparse pre-/post-flight visits.

## NASA OSD-569 — Inspiration4 complete blood count

https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569

Earlier exploratory astronaut biomarker model; sparse visits.

## NASA LSDA — Bed Rest Standard Measures: Cardiovascular Function

https://nlsp.nasa.gov/search/data/details/9af6f9ba-3730-54ab-a82a-1a32798667ae?dataType=EXPERIMENTS

Experiment context for current ground-analogue cardiovascular heads.

## NASA LSDA — UTMB C1 echocardiography

https://nlsp.nasa.gov/search/data/details/95186651-28d7-5c72-8f32-d40838766078?dataType=DATASETS

Current visit-based cardiac/vascular research heads.

## NASA LSDA — UTMB C3 echocardiography

https://nlsp.nasa.gov/search/data/details/c3bdfa7e-6661-5891-9ba0-0fc7c7a080d6?dataType=DATASETS

Current visit-based cardiac head.

## NASA LSDA — CFT70 cardiovascular measurements

https://nlsp.nasa.gov/search/data/details/59501092-7252-5848-b79c-650e954b51ed?dataType=DATASETS

Current 2D/3D cardiac heads; overlapping people are not counted twice.

## NASA OSDR RadLab — ISS DosTel1 environmental radiation

https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/data-api/

Current environmental radiation forecast head; not personal dosimetry.

## BIG IDEAs glycemic wearable dataset v1.1.3

https://physionet.org/content/big-ideas-glycemic-wearable/1.1.3/

Earth-based glucose, heart-rate and meal research; current continuous demonstration.

## PhysioNet Challenge 2019 v1.0.0

https://physionet.org/content/challenge-2019/1.0.0/

Earlier terrestrial forecasting benchmarks, not the current astronaut or wearable data.

## CGMacros v1.0.0

https://physionet.org/content/cgmacros/1.0.0/

Audited during data research; excluded from the current forecasting campaign because of unresolved interpolation concerns.

## Canadian Space Agency — monitoring radiation and astronaut health

https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp

Background on the mission-health monitoring problem; not a paired training dataset.
