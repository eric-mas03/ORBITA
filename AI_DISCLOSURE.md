# AI / SI assistance disclosure

ORBITA was developed with human direction and AI/SI assistance from OpenAI Codex. Assistance covered research planning, data-processing and evaluation code, forecasting implementation, software/debugging, explanatory text, the interface, diagrams and programmatic video composition.

Measured observations originate in the cited research datasets. They were not fabricated by a generative model. Derived windows, masks, summaries, forecasts and evaluation artifacts were produced by the project's AI-assisted analysis code. Forecasts are model outputs and remain distinguished from observed values.

The presentation uses programmatically generated, SI-assisted visuals and a visibly labelled SI-generated video. Neural-network particles are a conceptual illustration, not an empirical patient embedding. No NASA logo, flag or mission patch was generated or modified for the presentation. NASA source names are used for research attribution.

English narration is synthetic Microsoft Edge neural speech accessed through edge-tts. Available editions use Ava, Andrew, Ryan and Guy; the exact voice identifier is in each MP4's descriptive metadata and in `presentation/voice-editions/`. Original procedural sound effects were generated by code; no third-party music was used.

The research chatbot requires the visitor’s own signed-in Codex, Claude Code or OpenCode CLI, paired through a loopback connector. No author account or provider credential is included in the public site. It is not a fine-tuned clinical LLM and does not provide validated treatment recommendations or causal food/exercise simulations.

Machine-readable disclosures are provided in `data/manifests/si_assistance.json`, the voice-edition manifests and the submission video metadata. Submission text includes the same disclosure. Team members should add any other tools or resources they personally used and verify the final form before submitting.
